⚡ How to Use TappyThing
🎙️

1. Speak or Type Naturally

Tap Talk or Type. Say customer names, job notes, or tasks (e.g. "Pick up filters tomorrow for John").

🧾

2. Mention Any Dollar Amount

Say or type any price (e.g. "Tune up vacuum $120") to automatically generate an instant, 1-tap invoice card!

📷

3. Snap Photo Records

Tap Photo/Video to capture machines, parts, receipts, or job sites right into your cards.

🌍

4. Check 'My World'

Tap My World at the bottom anytime to see all your live shop records, workbench tickets, and bills!
